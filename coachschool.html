Reading the chalk

  • Dotted line: the route he is about to run.
  • Solid line: the part he has already run.
  • Blue zone: a deep defender's area.
  • Yellow zone: an underneath defender's area.
  • Blue line: the line of scrimmage.
  • Yellow line: the first-down marker.
