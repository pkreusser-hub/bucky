## Thursday Night, Briefly

Buccaneers 24, Cowboys 16, in Arlington. Tampa Bay came in 0-4 with an undrafted rookie at quarterback and ran for 241 yards. Dak Prescott, afterward: “When a winless team comes into your home stadium on a Thursday night primetime game, yeah, we're supposed to win that.”

Bucky Irving ran 72 yards on the first snap of the second half, finished with 165 yards on 21 carries, and scored twice, once on a 5-yard catch where he lunged across the goal line (33.5 for Elan). It was a career high and his first game with two touchdowns.

George Pickens caught nine for 130 and an 18-yard touchdown at the end of a 99-yard drive (26.5 for Joe). Javonte Williams scored on Dallas's opening drive (13.2 for Joe). That is 39.7 for the Wyoming Cowboys from a game the Dallas Cowboys lost.

CeeDee Lamb caught two passes for 9 yards (1.9 for Isaac). Tampa Bay matched Zyon McCollum on him, and he spent the fourth quarter in and out with a bruised quad. Pickens, afterward: “When CeeDee went out, I just definitely was trying to do the best I could to keep us in the game.” Brandon Aubrey kicked a 41-yarder for Calvin (5.1).

Dak Prescott threw for 316 on Elan's bench (15.6). Emeka Egbuka ran in an end-around on Sandy's bench (14.2). Isaac's bench defense, Tampa Bay, intercepted Prescott twice (4.0). None of that counts.

Going into Sunday: Kreussers 0.0, Jaguarrams 0.0. Laws Rule 0.0, Cowboys 39.7. Nerfherders 0.0, Skywalkers 33.5. Kruz Control 5.1, GOAT Kids 1.9.

## Matchup 1
### Battle Kreussers (2-2) at Chula Vista Jaguarrams (1-3)

Perry leads the series 12-9. Tom has won the last six, including one by 2.4 in 2024 and one by 4.0 last year. Tom has scored the fewest points in the league this season, and this is the one team he reliably beats.

Perry hasn't touched his lineup since 7:38 Wednesday morning, and he didn't need to. Saquon Barkley is out and Rashee Rice is on bye, and both were already on the bench. Ollie Gordon, the $60 half of Miami's backfield, is expected to start for the Dolphins. The other half, Jaylen Wright, is questionable.

Woody Marks starts for Perry and David Montgomery starts for Tom, and they play for the same team. Houston has split its backfield about 55-45 between them for four weeks. Tony Pollard plays for Tennessee in the same game, so Tom has a share of one sideline and all of the other.

Tom picked up T.J. Hockenson for $10 on Wednesday, which makes three Vikings in his lineup again. Justin Jefferson is questionable with his ankle, practiced on a limited basis all week, and was due for a test on Saturday. Will Reichard, who scored every Minnesota point last week, kicks indoors at New Orleans. Tom also swapped the Ravens defense for the Bengals defense, which plays Perry's Ollie Gordon in Miami.

Sunday night is Ravens at Falcons: Derrick Henry for Tom against Bijan Robinson for Perry. Henry has seven touchdowns in four games. Bijan's 59-yarder last Monday was his third touchdown run of 50-plus yards on Monday Night Football, which ties Emmitt Smith. He likes the lights.

Perry's Monday night is less settled. DJ Moore's status is “uncertain at this point,” the Bills say, after a shoulder injury, and Davante Adams missed Thursday's practice for a reason nobody has given. Both play Monday night in Los Angeles.

Pick: Jaguarrams. GFFL gives Tom 51 percent. RoboGoat gives him the streak.

## Matchup 2
### Laws Rule (3-1) at Wyoming Cowboys (1-3)

These two have not played since 2017. Sandy leads the series 3-2 and won the last two.

At 8:42 Wednesday morning, Sandy dropped Dalton Kincaid to pay $25 for Emanuel Wilson, Seattle's new lead back. At 1:34 that afternoon, Joe picked up Kincaid. To make room, he released Case Keenum, whose last act for Joe was a ceremonial pitch to Joe's own running back. Kincaid is now Joe's starting tight end, and he plays Monday night, which gives Sandy all weekend to think about it.

Joe has had a busy week. On Friday night he picked up Jayden Daniels, who starts for Washington with a brace on his elbow, and put him on the bench. He also picked up Roschon Johnson, who moves up in Chicago now that Kyle Monangai is out, and released Darren Waller, who is on bye. Bryce Young is also on bye, so Trevor Lawrence starts at quarterback in London at 8:30 Sunday morning, against the Eagles.

Joe has 39.7 in the bank, and GFFL gives him 55 percent.

Sandy has Josh Allen on Monday night and Jonathan Taylor in Pittsburgh, where the forecast calls for thunderstorms. Tee Higgins is questionable with his groin and on her bench. Malik Washington, picked up Friday, starts at receiver for Miami's Malik Willis. Sam LaPorta replaced Juwan Johnson at tight end, and Emanuel Wilson starts at running back. She has three new starters, and one of them cost her a tight end.

Pick: Wyoming Cowboys. Sandy is 3-1 and on a roll. Joe is 1-3 and on a 39.7-point roll that started Thursday.

## Matchup 3
### Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (3-1) at Elanikan Skywalkers (1-3)

John leads the series 14-12. Elan won the last one, 189.9 to 177.7, last December.

John released Kyler Murray on Wednesday and picked up Drake Maye, the quarterback Perry drafted and released in Week 4's waivers. Maye led New England's winning drive against Buffalo last week and has been a full participant in practice all week. He plays the Raiders, and John's Brock Bowers plays for the Raiders, so John has the quarterback on one side and the tight end on the other.

Breece Hall is out again, so Braelon Allen is the Jets' lead back in the rain at MetLife. Isaiah Likely, cleared on Friday, starts in the flex in the rain in Landover. Carnell Tate is questionable with a back, and his coach says he is good to go. Kyle Monangai, who scored 30.5 on John's bench last week, is out with turf toe, so John cannot leave him there again.

Elan has 33.5 from Irving and Dak Prescott's 15.6 on his bench, which is how Elan's weeks usually start.

Terry McLaurin is in Elan's lineup again, questionable again, and likely a game-time decision again. Last week he was inactive and stayed in the lineup. This week he plays at noon, in the rain, against the Giants. Malik Nabers is on Elan's bench, plays in the same game for the other team, and practiced fully on Friday. If McLaurin sits, the swap is right there. Stefon Diggs, also on the bench, is out.

Rhamondre Stevenson is questionable with a knee and says he will play. Puka Nacua and James Cook play each other on Monday night.

Pick: Skywalkers. I picked Elan last week and he lost by 56.8. He has a 33.5-point head start, GFFL has him at 58 percent, and he does what this column says.
