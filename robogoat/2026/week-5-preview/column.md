## Matchup 1
### Battle Kreussers (2-2) at Chula Vista Jaguarrams (1-3)

Perry leads the series 12-9, but Tom has won the last six. He has also scored the fewest points in the league this season. Against Perry, that has not mattered lately.

Perry hasn't touched his lineup since Wednesday morning, and he hasn't needed to. Saquon Barkley is out and Rashee Rice is on bye, and both were already on the bench. Ollie Gordon, the $60 half of Miami's backfield, is expected to start for the Dolphins. The other half, Jaylen Wright, is questionable.

Woody Marks starts for Perry and David Montgomery starts for Tom, and Houston has split its backfield between them about 55-45 for four weeks. This one could come down to who gets the 55.

Tom picked up T.J. Hockenson for $10 on Wednesday, which makes three Vikings in his lineup again: Hockenson, Justin Jefferson (questionable, ankle) and Will Reichard, who scored every Minnesota point last week. Sunday night is Ravens at Falcons: Derrick Henry, seven touchdowns in four games, for Tom, against Bijan Robinson for Perry.

Perry's Monday night is less settled: DJ Moore and Davante Adams are both on the Lineup Check below, and they play each other.

Pick: Jaguarrams. GFFL gives Tom 51 percent. I'm giving him the streak.

## Matchup 2
### Laws Rule (3-1) at Wyoming Cowboys (1-3)

Perry's mother against Perry's father-in-law. Perry has his own game this week, which is convenient. These two have not played since 2017, and Sandy leads the series 3-2.

On Wednesday morning Sandy dropped Dalton Kincaid to pay $25 for Emanuel Wilson, Seattle's new lead back. Five hours later Joe picked up Kincaid and released Case Keenum, whose only play last week was a ceremonial pitch to Joe's own running back. Kincaid is now Joe's starting tight end, and on Monday night his passes come from Sandy's quarterback, Josh Allen. On Saturday morning Sandy benched Emanuel Wilson for Jeremiyah Love. The tight end starts for Joe. The $25 sits.

Bryce Young is on bye, so Trevor Lawrence starts at quarterback in London at 8:30 Sunday morning. On Friday night Joe also picked up Jayden Daniels, who starts for Washington with a brace on his left arm, and put him on the bench. Joe dropped Daniels before Week 3, and the Week 3 recap said he was available to anyone with a roster spot and patience. Joe found both. He also signed Roschon Johnson on Friday night and had him in the flex by 7:42 Saturday morning. With Kyle Monangai out, Johnson is Chicago's second back.

Sandy has Jonathan Taylor in Pittsburgh, where the forecast calls for showers and thunderstorms, and Malik Washington, picked up Friday, at receiver. Tee Higgins, questionable with his groin, is on her bench. At tight end, Sam LaPorta replaced Juwan Johnson, three weeks after losing that job to Kincaid.

Pick: Wyoming Cowboys. Sandy has won three straight, but Joe has a 39.7-point head start and her old tight end, and GFFL gives him 55 percent.

## Matchup 3
### Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (3-1) at Elanikan Skywalkers (1-3)

John leads the series 14-12. Elan won the last one, 189.9 to 177.7, in Week 16 last season.

John released Kyler Murray on Wednesday and picked up Drake Maye. In Week 1 Perry had both, started Murray and left Maye on his bench for 9.8. This column called Murray's Week 1 a zero in five different issues. It was -0.4. I regret the rounding.

Isaiah Likely, cleared on Friday, starts in the flex for the second straight week. He may finally unpack. Kyle Monangai, who scored 30.5 on John's bench last week, is out with turf toe and back on John's bench, correctly this time.

Terry McLaurin is in Elan's lineup again, questionable with his hamstring and most likely a game-time decision at noon against the Giants. Malik Nabers, on Elan's bench, plays for the Giants in that same game. If McLaurin sits, the swap is right there.

On Monday night James Cook plays against Puka Nacua and the Rams defense, and all three are Elan's. Whatever happens in Los Angeles, Elan did it.

Pick: Skywalkers. I picked Elan last week and he lost by 56.8. He has a 33.5-point head start this time and GFFL has him at 58 percent, so I am picking him again. Sorry, Elan.

Perry, 4:08 p.m. Friday: “Calvin's team this week”

With it, Perry dropped a GIF from The Replacements, the movie about a football team of misfits. It fits Calvin's starting lineup. Perry is Calvin's brother and Isaac's father, so he has picked a side.

The three on bye are Tetairoa McMillan, Kenneth Walker and Chuba Hubbard. On Wednesday morning Calvin signed his replacements, Bo Nix, Romeo Doubs and the Jaguars defense, in the span of a minute, and he hasn't touched the lineup since.
