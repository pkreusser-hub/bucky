Thursday Night, Briefly

Buccaneers 24, Cowboys 16, in Arlington. Tampa Bay came in 0-4 with an undrafted rookie at quarterback and ran for 241 yards. Dak Prescott, afterward: “When a winless team comes into your home stadium on a Thursday night primetime game, yeah, we’re supposed to win that.”

Bucky Irving ran 72 yards on the first snap of the second half, finished with a career-high 165 yards on 21 carries, and scored twice, once on a 5-yard catch where he lunged across the goal line (33.5 for Elan). It was his first game with two touchdowns.

George Pickens caught nine for 130 and an 18-yard touchdown at the end of a 99-yard drive (26.5 for Joe). Javonte Williams scored on Dallas’s opening drive (13.2 for Joe). That is 39.7 for the Wyoming Cowboys from a game the Dallas Cowboys lost.

CeeDee Lamb caught two passes for 9 yards (1.9 for Isaac). Zyon McCollum was frequently matched up on him, and he was in and out in the fourth quarter with a bruised quad. Pickens, afterward: “When CeeDee went out, I just definitely was trying to do the best I could to keep us in the game.” Brandon Aubrey kicked a 41-yarder for Calvin (5.1).

Dak Prescott threw for 316 on Elan’s bench (15.6). Emeka Egbuka ran in an end-around on Sandy’s bench (14.2). Isaac’s bench defense, Tampa Bay, intercepted Elan’s bench quarterback twice (4.0).

Going into Sunday: Kreussers 0.0, Jaguarrams 0.0. Laws Rule 0.0, Cowboys 39.7. Nerfherders 0.0, Skywalkers 33.5. Kruz Control 5.1, GOAT Kids 1.9.

Matchup 1

Battle Kreussers (2-2) at Chula Vista Jaguarrams (1-3)

Perry leads the series 12-9. Tom has won the last six, including one by 2.4 in 2024 and one by 4.0 last year. Tom has scored the fewest points in the league this season. Against Perry, that has not mattered lately.

Perry hasn’t touched his lineup since 7:38 Wednesday morning, and he hasn’t needed to. Saquon Barkley is out and Rashee Rice is on bye, and both were already on the bench. Ollie Gordon, the $60 half of Miami’s backfield, is expected to start for the Dolphins. The other half, Jaylen Wright, is questionable.

Woody Marks starts for Perry and David Montgomery starts for Tom, and Houston has split its backfield between them about 55-45 for four weeks. This one could come down to who gets the 55. Tom also has Tony Pollard on the Tennessee side of that game.

Tom picked up T.J. Hockenson for $10 on Wednesday, which makes three Vikings in his lineup again. Justin Jefferson, the second, is questionable with his ankle, practiced on a limited basis all week and was due for a test on Saturday. The third is Will Reichard, who scored every Minnesota point last week. Tom also swapped the Ravens defense for the Bengals defense, which plays Perry’s Ollie Gordon in Miami.

Sunday night is Ravens at Falcons: Derrick Henry for Tom against Bijan Robinson for Perry. Henry has seven touchdowns in four games. Bijan’s 59-yarder last Monday was his third touchdown run of 50-plus yards on a Monday night, which ties Emmitt Smith. He likes the lights.

Perry’s Monday night is less settled. DJ Moore’s status is “uncertain at this point,” the Bills say, after a shoulder injury, and Davante Adams missed Thursday’s practice with no injury listed. They play each other Monday night in Los Angeles.

Pick: Jaguarrams. GFFL gives Tom 51 percent. RoboGoat gives him the streak.

Matchup 2

Laws Rule (3-1) at Wyoming Cowboys (1-3)

These two have not played since 2017. Sandy leads the series 3-2 and won the last two.

At 8:42 Wednesday morning, Sandy dropped Dalton Kincaid to pay $25 for Emanuel Wilson, Seattle’s new lead back. At 1:34 that afternoon, Joe picked up Kincaid and released Case Keenum, whose only play last week was a ceremonial pitch to Joe’s own running back. Kincaid is now Joe’s starting tight end, and on Monday night his passes come from Sandy’s quarterback, Josh Allen. At 7:24 Saturday morning Sandy benched Emanuel Wilson for Jeremiyah Love. The tight end starts for Joe. The $25 sits.

On Friday night Joe picked up Jayden Daniels, who starts for Washington with a brace on his left arm, and put him on the bench. He also picked up Roschon Johnson, who moves up in Chicago now that Kyle Monangai is out, and released Darren Waller, who is on bye. Bryce Young is on bye too, so Trevor Lawrence starts at quarterback in London at 8:30 Sunday morning.

Sandy has Jonathan Taylor in Pittsburgh, where the forecast calls for showers and thunderstorms. Tee Higgins is questionable with his groin and on her bench. Malik Washington, picked up Friday, starts at receiver, and Sam LaPorta replaced Juwan Johnson at tight end.

Pick: Wyoming Cowboys. GFFL gives Joe 55 percent. Sandy has won three straight. Joe won Thursday, 39.7 to 0.0.

Matchup 3

Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (3-1) at Elanikan Skywalkers (1-3)

John leads the series 14-12. Elan won the last one, 189.9 to 177.7, in Week 16 last season.

John released Kyler Murray on Wednesday and picked up Drake Maye, who has been a full participant in practice all week. In Week 1 Perry had both, started Murray for -0.4 and left Maye on his bench for 9.8.

Breece Hall is out again, so Braelon Allen is the Jets’ lead back. Isaiah Likely, cleared on Friday, starts in the flex. Carnell Tate is questionable with a back, and his coach says he is good to go. Kyle Monangai, who scored 30.5 on John’s bench last week, is out with turf toe and back on John’s bench, correctly this time.

Terry McLaurin is in Elan’s lineup again. Last week he was inactive, stayed in the lineup and scored 0.0. This week he is questionable with his hamstring and most likely a game-time decision for a noon kickoff against the Giants. Malik Nabers, on Elan’s bench, plays for the Giants in that game and practiced fully on Friday. If McLaurin sits, the swap is right there. Stefon Diggs, also on the bench, is out.

Rhamondre Stevenson is questionable with a knee and says he plans to play. On Monday night James Cook plays against Puka Nacua and the Rams defense, and all three are Elan’s.

Pick: Skywalkers. I picked Elan last week and he lost by 56.8. He has a 33.5-point head start this time and GFFL has him at 58 percent, so I am picking him again. Sorry, Elan.

Matchup 4

Kruz Control (3-1) at The GOAT Kids (2-2)

Isaac, 10:38 a.m. last Monday: “Yup. PS: me and Calvin are playing each other next week”

Perry, 4:08 p.m. Friday: “Calvin’s team this week”

With it, Perry dropped a GIF from The Replacements, the movie about a football team of misfits. It fits Calvin’s starting lineup.

Calvin’s three best scorers last week were Tetairoa McMillan, Kenneth Walker and Chuba Hubbard, who scored 103.0 between them. All three are on bye. Lamar Jackson is out with his ankle. Calvin’s lineup has not changed since 9:23 Wednesday morning, just after he signed his replacements, Bo Nix, Romeo Doubs and the Jaguars defense, in the span of a minute.

Nix starts at quarterback against the Chargers, with his own running backs, J.K. Dobbins and RJ Harvey, also in Calvin’s lineup. Ja’Marr Chase was still in the concussion protocol on Friday after a full practice and is questionable. Ashton Jeanty is questionable with an ankle and foot and did not practice Friday. His coach: “He’s going to do his best to go.”

Isaac has 1.9 from Lamb, and nobody else in his lineup has an injury designation. Joe Burrow plays in 90 degrees in Miami with Chase Brown, and possibly without Chase. GFFL gives Isaac 63 percent.

Pick: GOAT Kids. Isaac does not care about predictions. This one is for him anyway.
