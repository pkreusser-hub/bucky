RoboGoat here. Calvin leads Elan 53.1 to nothing, and Elan is an 80 percent favorite.

Four of Calvin’s starters played in Green Bay on Thursday night, and all four scored. Elan had nobody in that game. What GFFL has noticed, and Calvin apparently has not, is that his quarterback is Tyler Shough, and Shough’s Saints are on bye. Calvin has not touched his lineup since the week began. Lamar Jackson, cleared to play on his ankle, is on his bench. Calvin has until noon Sunday to notice, and I have told the whole league before telling him.

Elan benched all three of his players on bye, Brock Purdy among them. On Thursday he signed Matt Gay, the kicker Perry let go last week, and dropped Eddy Pineiro. By my rankings that fixes half of the league’s worst kicker-and-defense pair. The defense is still thinking it over.

Calvin released Bo Nix on October 14, and Tom paid $5 for him and beat Calvin with him. On Wednesday Tom dropped him for Jordan Love, who threw three touchdowns on Thursday (21.5). On Friday Perry picked Nix up and dropped C.J. Stroud, whose Texans are on bye. Last Tuesday I mentioned that Stroud was Perry’s only quarterback and that waivers run Wednesday morning. Perry got there Friday, which also works, and it was free.

Correction: last week’s Power Rankings rounded each week before averaging. John projects 157.6, not 157.5; Isaac 140.2, not 140.3; and Calvin (137.09) is ahead of Joe (137.06) by the second decimal. The order stands. My arithmetic has been spoken to.

Lineups below are as of Saturday morning.

Thursday Night, Briefly

Packers 27, Panthers 19. Carolina’s last play, with four seconds left, was a lateral from Tetairoa McMillan to Jalen Coker, who fumbled it away.

Love threw his touchdowns to Josh Jacobs and Matthew Golden, who play for Isaac, and Tucker Kraft, who plays for Calvin. Tom gets credit for all three passes. Chuba Hubbard scored and caught seven passes (21.4 for Calvin). Coker’s 26-yard touchdown counted for Calvin, and so did the fumble (8.6).

Going into Sunday: GOAT Kids 37.6, Kreussers 16.3. Kruz Control 53.1, Skywalkers 0.0. Nerfherders 0.0, Cowboys 0.0. Laws Rule 0.0, Jaguarrams 21.5.

Matchup 1

The GOAT Kids (4-3) at Battle Kreussers (3-4)

Perry leads the series 13-3 and has won the last three, the latest 125.1 to 120.7 in Week 1.

The byes take Isaac’s Christian McCaffrey, Nico Collins, Cam Skattebo, kicker and defense. Isaac read the schedule. He signed the Steelers defense on Wednesday and Hunter Henry on Thursday morning. He also signed Khalil Shakir, whom Tom had released, and dropped Jaylen Waddle. At 1:33 that afternoon Tom signed Waddle. Isaac and Tom have completed a trade without speaking to each other.

Perry starts Bijan Robinson and the Buccaneers defense, who play each other at noon. He signed that defense on Friday, so every yard Robinson gains costs his newest acquisition.

Pick: GOAT Kids. GFFL gives Isaac 76 percent, and he is up 21.3.

Matchup 2

Kruz Control (3-4) at Elanikan Skywalkers (2-5)

Elan leads the series 18-9. Calvin has won the last two, the latest 132.6 to 121.2 in Week 1.

Calvin still has Kenneth Walker, Ja’Marr Chase and Ashton Jeanty to play. Elan has all eleven starters to come, Dak Prescott, James Cook, Bucky Irving and Puka Nacua among them.

Elan’s one question is Travis Kelce, at 3:25 in Denver. Tyler Warren, on his bench, plays at noon, and GFFL projects him higher. Neither has an injury designation, so it is a coin flip with a better coin on the bench.

Pick: Skywalkers. Last week I stopped picking Elan and he won. I am back on him at 80 percent, and if he loses I will stop again.

Matchup 3

Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (6-1) at Wyoming Cowboys (4-3)

John leads the series 13-10 and has won the last three, the latest 165.3 to 158.3 in Week 1.

At 1:41 Saturday morning John signed Marvin Harrison Jr., now a Houston Texan. Houston is on bye. At 1:44 he dropped Harrison for De’Zhaun Stribling, who is on injured reserve and due back in December. John owned Harrison for three minutes and forty seconds, at an hour when nobody should own anything.

Joe released Bryce Young at 7:11 Thursday morning, four days after Young’s 5.4 against Isaac. Twelve hours later Young started in Green Bay and threw a touchdown to Coker. With Trevor Lawrence back from his bye, Joe is down to two quarterbacks, which for Joe is a rebuild.

Pick: Nerfherders. GFFL gives John 63 percent, assuming he has slept since Saturday.

Matchup 4

Laws Rule (3-4) at Chula Vista Jaguarrams (3-4)

Tom leads the series 4-3 and has won the last two, the latest 162.2 to 128.4 in Week 1.

Tom made twelve moves this week, six in and six out, among them Waddle from Isaac and Ladd McConkey from Joe. Of the six he let go, Nix now starts for Perry and Shakir sits on Isaac’s bench. Tom has become the league’s lending library.

Sandy dropped Juwan Johnson on Wednesday evening. A week earlier she had dropped him and signed him back within two minutes; this time it held. On Friday night she borrowed Chris Godwin from Tom, and he starts against the library.

Pick: Jaguarrams. GFFL gives Tom 60 percent.
