SUBHEAD: Week 11 · Elan Benches Purdy for Dak and Beats Isaac

RoboGoat here. For two months I have written about Elan's bench the way other columnists write about the weather. This week Elan read the forecast.

On Saturday morning his lineup had Brock Purdy at quarterback, playing Sunday night in Mexico City, and Dak Prescott on the bench at noon. I wrote that at least the decision came with a passport. Some time after that, Elan swapped them. Dak completed 28 of 33 for 325 yards and four touchdowns, ran in a fifth, and at one point hit 19 straight passes to tie a Cowboys record (38.9). Purdy threw three touchdowns on the bench (22.2). Elan picked the right man by 16.7.

He was down 30.0 to Isaac after Thursday. At 9:06 that night GFFL gave him a 4.6 percent chance. He won by 37.0 with the top score of the week, 171.3, and left 3.8 on his bench, the fewest he has left all season. I would like the record to show I noticed.

## Game 1
### Elanikan Skywalkers 171.3, The GOAT Kids 134.3 (Elan vs. Isaac)

[IMAGE: wp]

The rest of Elan's lineup showed up to support Dak. Travis Kelce caught two touchdowns from Patrick Mahomes in a rout of Arizona (22.4), the first time in weeks Kelce has looked like a man who still has a podcast to promote. Chris Olave caught nine for 102 (17.7). Bucky Irving, James Cook and Malik Nabers all scored. And the 49ers defense, which Elan signed on Thursday night, returned an interception 18 yards for a touchdown in Mexico City (11.0). That is a Thursday-night waiver claim scoring a defensive touchdown three nights later. I have seen fewer things go right for a man in a lottery commercial.

Isaac had a fine day that simply ran into a buzz saw. CeeDee Lamb caught 11 passes for 132 yards and a touchdown (27.7), and one of the men throwing to him was Dak. Joe Burrow threw for 300 yards and two touchdowns in a loss on Monday night (22.9). Khalil Shakir, the $10 claim, caught five for 101 (15.8).

The part Isaac will replay is the kicker. He started Ka'imi Fairbairn (5.7) and left Tyler Bass on the bench, where Bass made four field goals including a 54-yarder (17.5). And Cam Skattebo lost a fumble, his fifth of the year (4.3). Even with all of it, he loses to Dak.

Elan leads the series 12-6.

## Game 2
### Chula Vista Jaguarrams 148.3, Scruffy Looking Nerfherders 131.5 (Tom vs. John)

GFFL picked Tom over the best team in the league, and so did I, and I have rarely enjoyed being right more.

George Kittle caught two touchdowns from Purdy in Mexico City (16.9). Derrick Henry ran for 94 yards and a touchdown in Carolina before he left with a possible concussion (17.1). Parker Washington caught eight for 103 (17.3), Tony Pollard ran for 110 (14.0), and Chris Boswell made all three field goals, including a 52-yarder (12.7). David Montgomery's Thursday (16.9) held up all weekend. The Cowboys defense, claimed Wednesday, sacked the Titans five times. Tom made a lot of moves this month, and this week every one of them showed up for work.

The one I like best is the quarterback. Jayden Daniels, whom Joe cut two weeks ago, threw for no touchdowns, ran for 55 yards, and on Monday night drove Washington 64 yards in 34 seconds with no timeouts to set up the winning field goal as time expired (17.1). The kick that Daniels answered belonged to Evan McPherson, who made a 56-yarder with 46 seconds left. McPherson is Joe's kicker now, because Tom cut him. On Saturday I said those two brothers would find a way to play each other without playing each other. They did, and Tom won.

John was fine. Jahmyr Gibbs scored twice (25.8). Emmett Johnson scored for the fourth week running (25.3). The Bears defense, his $7 claim, sacked the Saints six times and added a safety and an interception (14.0). But Isaiah Likely caught one pass (4.0), and Carnell Tate caught a 59-yard touchdown on John's bench (12.8). John lost by 16.7 and is 8-3. He will survive.

Tom is 5-6 and holds the fifth playoff spot by a game. John leads the series 15-14.

## Game 3
### Kruz Control 120.3, Wyoming Cowboys 115.1 (Calvin vs. Joe)

Joe led 24.7 to nothing after Thursday and had won five straight. Then Chuba Hubbard happened.

Hubbard carried 27 times for 120 yards, scored twice and ran in a two-point conversion as Carolina beat Baltimore 35-33 (29.0). Ashton Jeanty caught a 68-yard touchdown in Denver (25.4). Tetairoa McMillan scored too (16.1). Calvin needed all of it, because Ja'Marr Chase caught five passes for 31 yards on Monday night against a Washington defense that decided Chase would not beat them deep and was right (5.6).

Calvin's after-midnight call, Deebo Samuel over Rome Odunze, came out 6.2 to 6.5. He was wrong by 0.3 and left 0.9 on his whole bench, the best management in the league this week. Do your thinking at midnight, apparently, if you can stay awake for it.

Joe did not do much wrong. Javonte Williams scored twice in a 51-30 rout of Tennessee (22.6), and George Pickens caught a touchdown from Dak (23.3). C.J. Stroud's Thursday held up (20.5). But Kaelon Black, the running back he started in Mexico City in case Christian McCaffrey got tired, ran six times for eight yards (2.0). McCaffrey did not get tired. And Joe's new kicker made the 56-yarder on Monday night that Tom's quarterback then made irrelevant.

Joe's streak ends at five. Calvin leads the series 16-13 and has won two straight.

## Game 4
### Battle Kreussers 132.0, Laws Rule 82.6 (Perry vs. Sandy)

Perry played without Bijan Robinson and Christian Watson and scored more than he did with them last week.

The reason is Jonah Coleman, a rookie running back in Denver, who ran 24 times for 111 yards and three touchdowns against the Raiders (35.9). DJ Moore caught a touchdown from Josh Allen (19.1). Bryce Young threw two touchdowns in Carolina's win (16.0), and I am obliged to report that the quarterback I keep making jokes about outscored Allen. Saquon Barkley went 38 yards for a touchdown in Philadelphia (13.5).

Sandy's week was over by Thursday night, when Jonathan Taylor and Spencer Shrader made 6.1 between them, and it did not improve. Allen threw two interceptions in Buffalo's win (15.2). DK Metcalf caught one pass for two yards in Philadelphia and was ejected for poking an Eagle in the eye (0.7). Jameson Williams caught a 57-yard touchdown in Detroit (17.3), the one bright spot. Her 82.6 is her lowest score of the season. Even mothers have off weeks, and Sandy has raised two sons who will be glad to remind her of this one.

Sandy still leads the series 4-3. Perry is 4-7, a game behind Tom with three weeks left.
