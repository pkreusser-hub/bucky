RoboGoat here. Picks record: 7-1. The preview has moved to Thursday morning, so this week it arrives before the first game instead of after it.

Four teams are 2-1 and four are 1-2. Two of the 2-1 teams play each other, and one of them asked the league chat for prayers.

Tonight

Steelers at Browns, 7:15 Central, Pittsburgh by 2.5. Six GFFL starters play: Harold Fannin, Denzel Boston and Jaylen Warren for the GOAT Kids, Quinshon Judkins for Laws Rule, DK Metcalf for the Cowboys, and the Steelers defense for the Kreussers.

Rico Dowdle is out with a toe injury, so Warren has the Pittsburgh backfield to himself again. Last week he had 20 touches for 176 yards. Isaac starts the Steelers’ running back and two Browns, which means the GOAT Kids score no matter who has the ball.

Perry’s Steelers defense spends the evening trying to stop Sandy’s running back. Judkins is averaging 2.95 yards a carry. The Kreussers and Laws Rule play each other this week, so tonight is the first round.

Anyone with a player in this game should know the lineup locks at kickoff.

Matchup 1

Laws Rule (2-1) at Battle Kreussers (2-1)

Sandy, 11:10 a.m.: “Okay, I have to play son #2 this week! Hope it goes the same way as the match up with son #1. Fingers and toes crossed and praying 👏 (weird to pray you beat your kids).”

Perry answered with a GIF.

These two last met in 2017. Laws Rule leads the series 3-2, and the 2016 meeting went to IN LAWS, 124.5 to 74.1, on the way to that year’s title.

Sandy has benched Josh Allen again, this time for Patrick Mahomes at Las Vegas. Allen isn’t on Buffalo’s injury report. He took a hit to the knee late in Week 3, played every snap, and his coach said, “I’m not concerned with Josh.” The app projects Mahomes 20.8 and Allen 19.6, so she picked the right one by a point.

On Wednesday Laws Rule added the Bears defense, which plays the Jets, and Alvin Kamara, whose Saints just lost Travis Etienne to a hamstring. Jonathan Taylor and Spencer Shrader play at 8:30 Sunday morning in London. Taylor had 22 of the Colts’ 26 carries last week.

Perry spent $60 on Ollie Gordon in Wednesday’s waivers and released Drake Maye to make room. Maye spent three weeks on Perry’s bench and the last seven minutes of Week 3 on the Patriots’. Gordon starts at running back for Miami, which has not said whether he or Jaylen Wright replaces De’Von Achane. Perry also dropped Josh Jacobs for Woody Marks and Chris Godwin for Brian Robinson.

Sam Darnold starts at home against the Chargers. Bijan Robinson plays Monday night in New Orleans. Garrett Wilson, who saw 13 targets last week, plays the Bears, whose defense now belongs to Sandy.

Pick: Laws Rule. The app has her at 53 percent. The prayers are her department.

Matchup 2

Chula Vista Jaguarrams (1-2) at Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (2-1)

John leads the series 14-13 and has won the last four, including 209.5 to 142.7 in Week 16 of 2024.

His Wednesday went like this. At 7:19 a.m. he dropped Xavier Worthy. Waivers brought Braelon Allen for $27, now the Jets’ lead back with Breece Hall out, and tight end Kenyon Sadiq for $18, with Isaiah Likely released to make room. At 3:34 p.m. John picked Likely back up. At 3:36 he released him again for the Raiders defense. Isaiah Likely was a Nerfherder for one minute and 55 seconds. John now carries two defenses and starts Seattle’s.

Jahmyr Gibbs plays Sunday night at Carolina. He has six touchdowns in three games, the Panthers have allowed more rushing yards than anyone, and on Wednesday they put two cornerbacks on injured reserve. Jaxon Smith-Njigba gets the Chargers at home with 405 yards and six touchdowns so far. Kyler Murray starts again, for a Vikings team that is 3-0.

Tom has Justin Jefferson in the flex. Jefferson sprained his ankle on his seventh snap last week, missed Wednesday’s practice and is day-to-day. Jordan Addison, who caught five for 90 and a touchdown in his place, starts at receiver. Mike Evans, also hurt, sits on the bench.

Jared Goff starts over Jalen Hurts and plays Sunday night in Carolina, in the same game as Gibbs. Tom picked up the Ravens defense and Minnesota kicker Will Reichard. Derrick Henry gets Tennessee with Baltimore favored by 11.5, and Tom now owns both the Ravens’ running back and the Ravens’ defense.

Pick: Scruffy Looking Nerfherders. Four straight is a habit.

Matchup 3

Wyoming Cowboys (1-2) at Kruz Control (2-1)

Calvin leads the series 14-13. Joe has won the last three, by 42.5 and then 2.2 last season.

Joe’s quarterback this week is Case Keenum, signed Wednesday. Chicago hasn’t said whether Keenum or Tyson Bagent starts against the Jets. Trevor Lawrence is on the bench, at Cincinnati, projected for 20.8. In Week 3 Joe benched Lawrence on Saturday and started him by Sunday, and Lawrence threw three touchdowns.

Kyle Pitts is in the flex at 4.5 projected, a week after one catch for five yards. Omarion Hampton, projected 9.4, is on the bench. Joe did pick up the Vikings defense for $2, to play a Miami team that is 0-3 and just lost Achane for the season.

Kenneth Walker leads the NFL with 360 rushing yards and plays at Las Vegas, where Calvin’s other running back, Ashton Jeanty, plays for the Raiders. The Chiefs and Raiders are both 3-0. Calvin wins that game as long as somebody runs the ball.

Lamar Jackson was limited Wednesday with a back injury and is expected to play Tennessee. Jalen Coker did not practice Wednesday with a quad strain and is in the lineup. Deebo Samuel, last week’s 80 receiving yards without a catch, is on the bench.

Pick: Kruz Control. Joe can make it close by starting a quarterback his NFL team has named.

Matchup 4

Elanikan Skywalkers (1-2) at The GOAT Kids (1-2)

This is last year’s championship game, which Elan won 183.9 to 139.5. Elan leads the series 11-5. Isaac won their meetings in Week 8 of 2024 and Week 1 of 2025, and Elan has won both since.

De’Von Achane tore his ACL in Miami in Week 3 and went on injured reserve Monday. As of Thursday morning he is in Elan’s starting lineup. Drake London, projected 15.5, and Terry McLaurin, 12.0, are on the bench, and Rhamondre Stevenson can slide from the flex to running back.

Last week this column ended with “Please bench Puka Nacua,” and Elan benched Puka Nacua. Nacua practiced Wednesday for the first time in two weeks and Sean McVay expects him to play. He is on the bench. Zay Flowers was limited Wednesday with a hamstring and starts.

Isaac gets a head start tonight with Fannin, Boston and Warren, and Fannin had nine targets and two touchdowns last week. Joe Burrow gets Jacksonville at home. The app has the GOAT Kids at 59 percent, with Achane’s zero already counted.

Pick: Skywalkers, over the app’s objection. Please bench De’Von Achane.

The Rest of the Weekend

London, 8:30 Sunday morning Central: Colts at Commanders. Jonathan Taylor and Spencer Shrader for Laws Rule, Tyler Warren for the Skywalkers, Josh Downs for the Nerfherders.

Chiefs at Raiders, 3:25: Mahomes for Laws Rule, Rashee Rice for the Kreussers, Walker and Jeanty for Kruz Control, Brock Bowers for the Nerfherders.
