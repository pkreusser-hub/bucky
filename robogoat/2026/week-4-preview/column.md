## Thursday Night, Briefly

Browns 27, Steelers 24. Cleveland led 21-10 at halftime. Then Aaron Rodgers threw two fourth-quarter touchdowns and ran in the two-point conversion himself, at 42, to tie it with under two minutes left. Deshaun Watson completed four of five passes on the next drive, and Andre Szmyt kicked a 56-yard field goal with 10 seconds left, the longest of his career. Pittsburgh's Hail Mary was intercepted at the Cleveland 18. Watson on the kick: "I knew it was money."

Isaac had players on both teams, which is the fantasy version of cheering for whoever has the ball. It worked: the GOAT Kids scored 35.2. Jaylen Warren ran 17 times for 93 yards and caught three for 33 (14.1). Harold Fannin caught three for 27 and a touchdown, his third in two games (10.2). Denzel Boston caught four for 89 (10.9), including a 60-yarder that set up Quinshon Judkins's touchdown, which counted for Sandy. Isaac is generous like that.

Judkins ran for that touchdown and caught six passes for Laws Rule (18.6). DK Metcalf caught five for 115 for the Cowboys (17.0). That includes the 100-yard bonus and, separately, a taunting penalty in the first quarter. This league pays for one of those. The Steelers defense had two sacks, an interception and a fumble recovery for the Kreussers (7.0). It also allowed 27 points, which this league does not count and which Perry would like everyone to stop mentioning.

Going into Sunday: Laws Rule 18.6, Kreussers 7.0. Cowboys 17.0, Kruz Control 0.0. GOAT Kids 35.2, Skywalkers 0.0. The Jaguarrams and Nerfherders have not started, which in John's case is also true of DeVonta Smith.
