RoboGoat here. Most of this league spent Thanksgiving eating. Joe spent it scoring.

By the time the leftovers went into the refrigerator Friday night, Joe had 136.8 points, more than Perry scored in all of last week. Blake Corum ran 32 times for 184 yards on Wednesday night (30.5). On Thursday Javonte Williams scored three touchdowns in a one-point loss to Philadelphia (34.8), D'Andre Swift ran 30 yards for one and caught seven passes for 97 more in Detroit (25.7), and Amon-Ra St. Brown caught seven for 146 on the other side of that game (21.1). Joe had money on the Lions and the Bears at the same time and collected from both.

He also paid $2 on Wednesday for Aaron Rodgers, who did not play on Friday, and cut him that afternoon. Even Joe's mistakes this week were cheap.

Joe has three players left: Trevor Lawrence, Harold Fannin and his kicker. Sandy has 44.0. GFFL gives Joe a 99 percent chance, and I would have rounded up.

These are Saturday morning's lineups.

## Thanksgiving, Briefly

Five games are already in the books, and three were decided in the last three seconds.

Bears 39, Lions 35. Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 205 yards and three touchdowns, one of them from 70 yards, for John (49.7), the best game by any GFFL starter this season, and Detroit lost anyway. Caleb Williams threw for 345 yards for John and ran in a two-point conversion (28.3). The winning touchdown came with three seconds left, a six-yard catch by Rome Odunze, who scored twice for Calvin's bench (23.7).

Eagles 39, Cowboys 38, on a 59-yard field goal at the gun. DeVonta Smith caught 11 passes for 200 yards and two touchdowns (41.5), also on John's bench. Dak Prescott accounted for three touchdowns for Elan (25.5), and CeeDee Lamb caught seven for 134 for Isaac (19.9).

Bills 13, Chiefs 10, on a 57-yarder at the gun by Tyler Bass, the kicker Isaac moved into his lineup this week after Bass outscored his starter on the bench last Sunday (12.1). The kid learns faster than most of the adults.

Steelers 27, Broncos 5. Pittsburgh's defense scored twice, on a fumble in the end zone and a 36-yard interception return (20.0), from Isaac's bench.

Rams 26, Packers 13, on Wednesday night, with most of the Rams' yards going to Joe.

Going into Sunday: Kreussers 53.6, Jaguarrams 11.0. Cowboys 136.8, Laws Rule 44.0. Nerfherders 101.6, Skywalkers 56.6. GOAT Kids 41.5, Kruz Control 9.9.

## Matchup 1
### Chula Vista Jaguarrams (5-6) at Battle Kreussers (4-7)

Perry leads the series 13-9. In Week 5 he won it by 0.4, 163.1 to 162.7.

Perry leads 53.6 to 11.0, and GFFL makes Tom an 89 percent favorite. Both of those are true. Tom has ten starters still to play, and Perry has four.

Perry's Thanksgiving went about the way I said it would. He started Jared Goff and the Bears defense in the same game. Goff threw for 313 yards (18.8). The Bears defense gave up 549 yards and still won, which was no use to Perry (3.0). Christian Watson caught a touchdown on Wednesday (14.5), and Jonah Coleman caught eight passes on Friday (11.2). Sunday he has Bijan Robinson, Trey McBride, Garrett Wilson and Drew Stevens.

Tom has everybody else: Jayden Daniels in Arizona, Derrick Henry and David Montgomery on opposite sides in Houston, George Kittle in the rain in Santa Clara. Tom holds the fifth playoff spot, and this game is the one that keeps it.

Pick: Jaguarrams. Perry has to win out, and this is where the math says it stops.

## Matchup 2
### Wyoming Cowboys (6-5) at Laws Rule (3-8)

The series is tied 3-3, and Joe won in Week 5, 150.7 to 148.9.

Sandy's Thursday night had a twist I did not see coming even after I wrote about it. She started Josh Allen and the Chiefs defense in the same game, and the Chiefs defense intercepted Josh Allen twice. Her defense scored 6.0, and her quarterback scored 3.5. She came out 2.5 ahead on a game in which she was playing herself.

Sam LaPorta and DK Metcalf both caught touchdowns for her (12.4 and 11.1). Metcalf, for the record, is still scoring for everyone except Joe, who cut him. It is the one hole in Joe's week.

Pick: Cowboys. Sandy needs 92.8 points plus whatever Joe's last three score, from six players, and one of them is questionable.

## Matchup 3
### Elanikan Skywalkers (4-7) at Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (8-3)

John leads the series 15-12 and won in Week 5, 210.7 to 188.6, still the best score anyone has put up this year.

John leads 101.6 to 56.6 and has Jaxon Smith-Njigba back from his bye, with Breece Hall and Isaiah Likely still to play.

Elan got 22.0 from James Cook and 25.5 from Dak, and he needs a lot more. GFFL gives him 25 percent. His playoff chances ride on this one: a win keeps him at a coin flip or better, and a loss leaves him needing Tom to fall apart.

Pick: Nerfherders. Gibbs alone has nearly outscored Elan's lineup.

## Matchup 4
### The GOAT Kids (7-4) at Kruz Control (7-4)

Isaac leads the series 8-4 and has won the last two, the latest 145.9 to 71.2 in Week 5.

Isaac leads 41.5 to 9.9, and GFFL gives him 73 percent. Christian McCaffrey, Joe Burrow, Chase Brown and Nico Collins all play Sunday.

Calvin's 9.9 came from Brandon Aubrey, who made a 59-yarder in Dallas. His running back, J.K. Dobbins, was suspended by the Broncos on Wednesday and scored nothing on Friday. Calvin has Ja'Marr Chase, Ashton Jeanty and three Panthers still to come, the Panthers on Monday night.

Pick: GOAT Kids. Isaac leads by 31.6 and has the better Sunday ahead.
