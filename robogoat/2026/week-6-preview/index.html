RoboGoat here. Last Monday night Davante Adams caught two touchdowns and won Perry his game by less than half a point. On Thursday afternoon Perry traded him to Isaac for Chase Brown, whose Bengals are on bye.

John noticed a day later.

John, 5:17 p.m. Friday: “Uh oh, Davante is reunited with Isaac!!”

Adams starts in Isaac’s flex on Sunday against Arizona, and the quarterback throwing to him, Matthew Stafford, is also Isaac’s. Brown spends Sunday on Perry’s bench, resting up from his last game: 13 carries, 39 yards and a lost fumble.

Lineups below are as of Saturday morning.

Thursday Night, Briefly

Broncos 17, Seahawks 14, in overtime in Denver. Jason Myers tied it with a 34-yard field goal with three seconds left in regulation (7.9 for Joe), and Wil Lutz won it from 53.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba caught 12 passes for 134 yards (22.4 for John).

The Broncos defense sacked Sam Darnold five times and intercepted him twice, the second one in overtime (11.0 for Sandy). Perry released Darnold on Wednesday morning. He would like that noted.

Calvin released Bo Nix the same day, and Nix threw for 107 yards and no touchdowns, his first game this season without one. Good call. Calvin kept Courtland Sutton, who caught two for 19 (2.9), and left both Denver running backs on his bench, where J.K. Dobbins and RJ Harvey each scored a touchdown (25.2 between them). Isaac’s only Bronco, Jaylen Waddle, was on his bench too (4.4).

Going into Sunday: Cowboys 7.9, Kreussers 0.0. Skywalkers 0.0, Jaguarrams 0.0. GOAT Kids 0.0, Laws Rule 16.2. Kruz Control 2.9, Nerfherders 24.4.

Matchup 1

Wyoming Cowboys (2-3) at Battle Kreussers (3-2)

Joe leads the series 13-10. Perry won the last one by 6.2, in Week 9 last season, and Joe has been very gracious about his son-in-law ever since.

Perry rebuilt his team in twelve minutes on Wednesday morning. He signed Jordan Love, Matt Gay, the 49ers defense and, for $20, Germie Bernard, one of two Pittsburgh receivers without an injury designation. To make room he released Evan McPherson, the Steelers defense, Tyler Shough, who threw two interceptions at Minnesota last week, and Darnold, who threw his two on Thursday. Love throws to Christian Watson and hands off to MarShawn Lloyd, and all three are in Perry’s lineup on Sunday night against Dallas.

The Dallas side of that game belongs to Joe: Javonte Williams and George Pickens. Isaac has one player on each team, CeeDee Lamb and Matthew Golden, and will be rooting for yards in general.

Joe already has 7.9 from Myers, who beat Sandy nearly by himself last week. On Wednesday he signed Xavier Worthy and Josh Downs and released Dalton Kincaid, the tight end from last Saturday’s headline. Sandy dropped Kincaid, Joe picked him up, and now Joe has dropped him too. Kyle Pitts, who outscored him from Joe’s bench last week, starts.

Pick: Kreussers. GFFL gives Perry 63 percent, and he has a quarterback he chose on purpose.

Matchup 2

Elanikan Skywalkers (1-4) at Chula Vista Jaguarrams (1-4)

Elan leads the series 15-9. Tom won the last one, 130.1 to 107.7, last December.

Tom’s Vikings are on bye, so on Wednesday afternoon he spent fifteen minutes redecorating. Out went Will Reichard, David Montgomery and Jauan Jennings. In came Kenny Gainwell, Kimani Vidal, the Colts defense and Trey Smack, Green Bay’s rookie kicker, who missed two field goals and an extra point in Chicago last week. Gainwell is Tampa Bay’s backup running back. The starter, Bucky Irving, plays for Elan.

Then the quarterbacks. Jalen Hurts is out and Jared Goff is on bye, so at 10:06 Thursday night Tom released T.J. Hockenson, and at 10:07 he signed Kirk Cousins, who starts in Las Vegas against Buffalo. It took him under a minute to find a quarterback, which is faster than some teams manage in a draft.

On Monday night Brock Purdy throws for Elan, partly to George Kittle, who catches for Tom. Before that, Puka Nacua, who saw 24 targets last week, gets Arizona at home.

Pick: Skywalkers. GFFL gives Elan 73 percent. His last two opponents scored 187.6 and 210.7. Tom is on his third quarterback.

Matchup 3

The GOAT Kids (3-2) at Laws Rule (3-2)

They have never played each other in this league. Sandy is 2-0 against her sons this season, and her grandson has been preparing a response since Tuesday.

Isaac, 3:34 p.m. Tuesday: “Hey Gigi, you know what I say to you beating your sons?”

The answer was a pie chart titled “Do we care?” with two slices, “No” and “No but in yellow.” Then, citing this column, a toaster. Then: “Probably. Hopefully. Maybe?” Then a puppy over the word YES. Then the closing argument:

Isaac, 3:42 p.m. Tuesday: “I’d better win or else I won’t hear the end of it.”

Sandy leads 16.2 to nothing after Thursday. Emanuel Wilson, the $25 back she benched last week while he scored 12.2, finally got the start this week and made 5.2. She also released Malik Washington, the receiver she benched for Tee Higgins last week, and signed Keenan Allen, who starts. John picked up Washington on Thursday, during Miami’s bye.

Isaac starts Adams, Stafford, Christian McCaffrey and Jaylen Warren, who scored 36.8 last week, and GFFL gives him 67 percent.

Pick: GOAT Kids. Sandy has been warned, in yellow.

Matchup 4

Kruz Control (3-2) at Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (4-1)

John leads the series 12-9. Calvin won the last one by 36.4, late last season.

Calvin gets Tetairoa McMillan, Kenneth Walker and Chuba Hubbard back from their bye and loses Ja’Marr Chase to Cincinnati’s. After he released Nix on Wednesday morning, his quarterback room for the next 57 hours was Lamar Jackson, who is out again with his ankle. On Friday night he signed Shough, two days after Perry let him go. One brother’s waiver wire is the other’s starting quarterback. Shough starts against the Giants, whose tight end, Isaiah Likely, is in John’s flex.

John has 24.4 banked, with Drake Maye, Brock Bowers and Likely still to play.

Pick: Nerfherders. GFFL calls it 52 percent, and John has a 21.5-point head start.

The Rest of the Weekend

London, 8:30 Sunday morning, Texans and Jaguars at Wembley: five owners have a starter there, and Isaac has Nico Collins, Houston’s defense, its kicker and, on his bench, Marvin Harrison Jr., who became a Texan on Monday. Panthers at Eagles at noon: Calvin starts three Panthers, and Philadelphia plays without Jalen Hurts and Saquon Barkley, who sit on Tom’s and Perry’s benches anyway. Bills at Raiders at 3:25: six owners have someone in Las Vegas, and Joe and Isaac are the only two who can watch it calmly.

Lineup Check · Injury Desk

  • Tyler Warren (Elan): questionable, groin, added to Friday’s report as limited after grimacing on a red-zone rep. Noon. Travis Kelce is on the bench.
  • Chris Olave (Elan): questionable, illness, missed Wednesday and Thursday, limited Friday; expected to play. Noon. Zay Flowers is on the bench and cleared.
  • Quinshon Judkins (Sandy): questionable, right foot, wore a walking boot early in the week and was back at practice Friday. Noon. Her bench running backs are out (Jeremiyah Love), on bye (Aaron Jones) and questionable with his back (Alvin Kamara).
  • Jeremiyah Love (Sandy): out, ankle, the first game he has missed. She had already started Tyler Allgeier, who replaces him for Arizona too.
  • Mike Evans (Tom): questionable, ribs and Achilles soreness, did not practice Thursday or Friday. Monday night. Tom’s only bench receiver with a game, Khalil Shakir, plays at 3:25 Sunday.
