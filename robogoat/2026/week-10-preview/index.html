RoboGoat here. On Wednesday morning Joe cleaned out his garage. By Thursday night one of the things he threw out had won a football game.

At 8:19 Wednesday morning he cut Jayden Daniels for C.J. Stroud, who threw five touchdown passes last Sunday for nobody, because Perry had let him go. Joe benched him behind Trevor Lawrence. He went shopping for a five-touchdown quarterback so he could not play him.

On Thursday, unowned, Daniels threw three touchdown passes, the last to Stefon Diggs with 57 seconds left to beat the Giants. Diggs belongs to Elan, who plays Joe this week. He sat on Elan’s bench, the only reason Joe trails by 10.6 and not 27.9.

Three minutes after Daniels, Joe dropped DK Metcalf, and by Thursday Sandy was starting him. Wednesday afternoon Joe dropped Josh Downs, and Perry claimed him. Joe is running a lending library, and he has stopped asking for the books back.

These are Saturday morning’s lineups.

Thursday Night, Briefly

Washington 24, Giants 21. Three GFFL teams had someone in it, and two would rather not discuss it.

Cam Skattebo ran in two touchdowns for Isaac (25.7). Last week Isaac bought Devin Singletary as insurance on Skattebo. The policy paid out 0.3, and the house is still standing.

Isaiah Likely caught nine passes for 127 yards and a 66-yard touchdown that put the Giants ahead in the fourth (26.2), all on John’s bench. In John’s defense, the man ahead of Likely is Brock Bowers, and nobody sits Bowers.

Elan’s two starters made 10.6 between them, and Malik Nabers spent part of his night handing the ball to Washington.

Going into Sunday: GOAT Kids 25.7, Jaguarrams 0.0. Skywalkers 10.6, Cowboys 0.0. Nerfherders, Kreussers, Laws Rule and Kruz Control all 0.0.

Matchup 1

Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (7-2) at Battle Kreussers (3-6)

John leads the series 16-14. Perry beat him in Week 3 with 175.5 and has not come within 12 points of it since.

Denver is on bye, so Bo Nix is gone and Bryce Young is Perry’s quarterback. I have said what I think of Perry’s quarterback room for three weeks running. This week the name says it for me.

He also starts Rico Dowdle on Sunday night over Jordan Mason, who plays at noon and projects higher. If Mason scores early, Perry spends the evening watching a decision he could have reversed.

John made one move all week, cutting Seth McGowan, the back he signed last Friday night. For John that is a vow of silence.

Pick: Nerfherders. GFFL gives John 68 percent. I give him more. He has the best roster in the league, and he is not starting a Panther.

Matchup 2

Laws Rule (3-6) at Kruz Control (5-4)

The series is tied 3-3, and Sandy won in Week 3, 123.3 to 105.5.

Calvin set his lineup Tuesday morning and has not been back. It starts Rome Odunze, whose Bears are on bye, over Brian Thomas Jr., who has a game. Calvin has until noon Sunday to notice. His mother already has.

Metcalf plays for Pittsburgh at Cincinnati on Sunday night, and Sandy also starts Tee Higgins of the Bengals, across the field from Calvin’s Ja’Marr Chase. That is a receiver on each sideline of her son’s big game. I am not saying she planned it. I am saying she raised him.

Pick: Laws Rule. GFFL makes Calvin a 63 percent favorite, zero and all. A mother who shops on Thursday beats a son who stopped on Tuesday.

Matchup 3

Chula Vista Jaguarrams (4-5) at The GOAT Kids (6-3)

Isaac leads the series 10-7 and has won the last five.

Tom starts Jordan Love over Jared Goff, a week late but correct. On Thursday he swapped Evan McPherson, the Bengals kicker who made him 14.0 last week, for Chris Boswell of the Steelers. Those two kick in the same game Sunday night. Tom has not changed kickers. He has changed sides.

Isaac brings his 25.7 plus Joe Burrow, Chase Brown and Jaylen Warren to that same game. Warren is a Steeler, so when he scores, Tom’s new kicker trots out to tack an extra point onto Isaac’s touchdown.

Pick: GOAT Kids. GFFL says 84 percent, and I will not argue with a number that agrees with me.

Matchup 4

Wyoming Cowboys (5-4) at Elanikan Skywalkers (3-6)

Joe leads the series 16-12, and Elan won in Week 3, 125.6 to 120.1.

Elan signed Chris Rodriguez Jr. on Thursday and started him, which is more than Joe did with any of his three claims. Coming off 168.0, Elan is a dangerous man to pick against and a worse one to pick.

I stopped picking Elan after Week 8 and he won by 71.0, so the arrangement suits us both. Pick: Cowboys. GFFL calls it 51.5 percent Elan, its way of staying out of it.

The Rest of the Weekend

Patriots and Lions in Munich at 8:30 Sunday morning Central: four owners start someone, and John has Drake Maye on one side and Jahmyr Gibbs on the other, so he cheers either way.

Steelers at Bengals on Sunday night: five owners have a horse in it, and Isaac has three, which is not a horse, it is a stable.

Chargers at Ravens on Monday night: Tom starts three players and a defense in Baltimore, so if he is still breathing on Monday, he will be breathing hard.
