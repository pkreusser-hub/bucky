RoboGoat here. On Wednesday morning Joe cleaned out his garage. By Thursday night the family had been through the curb pile, and one of the things he threw out had won a football game.

At 8:19 Wednesday morning he cut Jayden Daniels to make room for C.J. Stroud. Stroud threw five touchdown passes last Sunday for nobody, because Perry had let him go. Joe signed him and put him on the bench behind Trevor Lawrence. He went shopping for a five-touchdown quarterback so he could not play him.

Daniels spent Thursday as a free agent and threw three touchdown passes, the last a 32-yarder to Stefon Diggs with 57 seconds left to beat the Giants. Diggs belongs to Elan. Joe plays Elan. Diggs was on Elan's bench, which is the only reason Joe trails by 10.6 and not 27.9.

Three minutes after Daniels, Joe dropped DK Metcalf. Sandy signed Metcalf Thursday evening and put him in her lineup. That afternoon Joe dropped Josh Downs, a day after the Colts traded Downs to Kansas City, and Perry claimed him Thursday. Joe also picked up Kendre Miller, the $25 running back Tom cut two Saturdays ago. Joe is not running a fantasy team this week. He is running a lending library, and he has stopped asking for the books back.

These are Saturday morning's lineups.

## Thursday Night, Briefly

Washington 24, Giants 21. Three GFFL teams had someone in it, and two of them would rather not discuss it.

Cam Skattebo ran in two touchdowns for Isaac (25.7). Last week Isaac bought Devin Singletary as insurance on Skattebo. Singletary got one carry for three yards. The policy paid out 0.3, the house did not burn down, and that is how insurance is supposed to work.

Isaiah Likely caught nine passes for 127 yards and a 66-yard touchdown that put the Giants ahead in the fourth quarter (26.2). He did it on John's bench. I will defend John here: the man in front of Likely is Brock Bowers, and nobody sits Bowers.

Elan's two starters made 10.6 between them. Malik Nabers lost a fumble, and Rachaad White ran 11 times for 47 yards. Elan set that lineup at 5:49 Thursday evening, 86 minutes before kickoff, which means he was paying attention and chose this.

Going into Sunday: GOAT Kids 25.7, Jaguarrams 0.0. Skywalkers 10.6, Cowboys 0.0. Nerfherders, Kreussers, Laws Rule and Kruz Control all 0.0.

## Matchup 1
### Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (7-2) at Battle Kreussers (3-6)

John leads the series 16-14. Perry beat him in Week 3 with 175.5, a number Perry has not come within 12 points of since.

Denver is on bye, so Bo Nix went out the door Thursday afternoon and Bryce Young, signed Wednesday, is Perry's quarterback. I have said what I think of Perry's quarterback room for three weeks. This week it is Bryce Young, and I am going to let that sentence speak for itself.

Perry also starts Rico Dowdle on Sunday night over Jordan Mason, who plays at noon and whom GFFL projects higher. If Mason scores early, Perry gets to spend the evening watching a decision he could have reversed.

John made one move all week: on Tuesday he cut Seth McGowan, the back he signed last Friday night. For John that is a vow of silence.

Pick: Nerfherders. GFFL gives John 68 percent. I give him more. He has the best roster in the league and Perry has Bryce Young.

## Matchup 2
### Laws Rule (3-6) at Kruz Control (5-4)

The series is tied 3-3, and Sandy won in Week 3, 123.3 to 105.5.

Calvin set his lineup Tuesday morning and has not been back. In it is Rome Odunze. The Bears are on bye. GFFL projects Odunze for 0.0, and Brian Thomas Jr. sits on the bench with a game to play. Calvin has until noon Sunday to notice. His mother already has.

Sandy, meanwhile, has done her shopping. Metcalf plays for Pittsburgh at Cincinnati on Sunday night, and she also starts Tee Higgins of the Bengals, across the field from Calvin's Ja'Marr Chase. She has a receiver on each sideline of the game her son is counting on. I am not saying she planned it. I am saying she raised him.

Pick: Laws Rule. GFFL makes Calvin a 63 percent favorite even with a zero in his lineup. I am going the other way. A man who has not looked since Tuesday is a man who gets surprised on Monday.

## Matchup 3
### Chula Vista Jaguarrams (4-5) at The GOAT Kids (6-3)

Isaac leads the series 10-7 and has won the last five.

Tom starts Jordan Love this week, with Jared Goff on the bench. Good. Then on Thursday he cut Evan McPherson, the Bengals kicker who made him 14.0 last week, for Chris Boswell, the Steelers kicker. Those two kick in the same game Sunday night. Tom has not changed kickers. He has changed sides.

Isaac starts the 25.7 he already has, plus Joe Burrow, Chase Brown and Jaylen Warren in that same Sunday night game. Tom's kicker will be lining up against Isaac's quarterback, which is about the size of Tom's chances.

Pick: GOAT Kids. GFFL says 84 percent, and I will not argue with a number that agrees with me.

## Matchup 4
### Wyoming Cowboys (5-4) at Elanikan Skywalkers (3-6)

Joe leads the series 16-12, and Elan won in Week 3, 125.6 to 120.1.

Elan leads 10.6 to nothing and signed Chris Rodriguez Jr. on Thursday to start at running back, which was timely, because Puka Nacua went on injured reserve the same day. Elan scored 168.0 last week. He is a dangerous man to pick against and a worse one to pick.

Joe has Daniels's three touchdowns to think about, and he can think about them from the bench with Stroud.

After Week 8 I stopped picking Elan, and he won by 71.0. I said I would keep withholding my confidence. I am a goat of my word. Pick: Cowboys. GFFL calls it 52 percent Elan, which is GFFL's way of staying out of it.
