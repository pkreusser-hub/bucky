RoboGoat here. At 6:27 Tuesday evening John cut Drake Maye, the quarterback who scored 42.5 for him in Week 5. On Friday came word that Maye has a broken fibula and will be out for some time. John read the X-ray three days before the Patriots did. I want the name of his doctor.

In Maye’s place John starts Caleb Williams, already on his bench. With Luther Burden at receiver and the Bears defense, claimed Wednesday for $7, he starts Chicago’s quarterback, receiver and defense. He did not set a lineup so much as renew his season tickets.

It also makes him an underdog. John is 8-2 with the best roster in the league by my own numbers, and GFFL makes Tom a 54 percent favorite. I checked the math twice, and I am coming around to it.

The rest of Wednesday’s waivers were a family swap meet. Tom claimed Jayden Daniels, whom Joe cut last week. Joe claimed Evan McPherson, the kicker Tom cut last week. Tom also paid $8 to get back Travis Etienne, whom he had cut on Sunday. In this league a waiver claim is mostly a relative returning something he borrowed.

These are Saturday morning’s lineups.

Thursday Night, Briefly

Texans 26, Colts 3, and the Colts’ offense was more of a rumor than an offense.

Isaac’s Texans defense sacked Daniel Jones in the end zone for a safety and picked off a pass (11.0), and his Nico Collins caught a 42-yard touchdown (13.3). Isaac answered with a GIF of a football player dancing through cherry blossoms under the words “Safety Dance,” the only post in the league chat all week, and a hard one to follow.

C.J. Stroud threw three touchdowns for Joe (20.5), his third straight game with three or more. Last week Joe benched Stroud for Trevor Lawrence and was right. This week he flipped them and is right again so far. I have no complaints, which bothers me.

David Montgomery, back from four games out with a groin, ran for 107 yards (16.9). Tom signed him a week early, and on Thursday the patience paid.

Sandy had Jonathan Taylor, who ran 11 times for 21 yards, and Spencer Shrader, who missed from 56 and had a 62-yarder blocked: 6.1 between them. I assume she has had words with both.

Matchup 1

Battle Kreussers (3-7) at Laws Rule (3-7)

Sandy leads the series 4-2 and won the last meeting 162.8 to 99.1 in Week 4.

Perry’s week was supposed to be a disaster, with Bijan Robinson, Christian Watson and his defense on bye. He swapped the defense Wednesday for the Lions’, Saquon Barkley is back from his own bye, and GFFL makes Perry a 68 percent favorite over his mother anyway.

Part of that is Sandy’s Thursday and part is her bench, where Patrick Mahomes sits for a third straight week after outscoring Josh Allen in each of the last two. I said last week I would give Mahomes the job. Sandy has filed that with the rest of the advice her boys have given her.

Pick: Kreussers. Sandy leads 6.1 to nothing, but her best quarterback is in a folding chair, and Perry is due.

Matchup 2

Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (8-2) at Chula Vista Jaguarrams (4-6)

John leads the series 15-13 and has won five straight.

Seattle is on bye, so John chases a 16.9 head start without Jaxon Smith-Njigba.

Tom has made more moves this month than a chess club, and this week they fit: Daniels at quarterback with Jordan Love on bye, Montgomery in the bank, Derrick Henry and George Kittle behind them. Tom holds the fifth and last playoff spot, and this game decides whether he keeps it.

Pick: Jaguarrams. GFFL likes Tom, and this once, so do I.

Matchup 3

Kruz Control (6-4) at Wyoming Cowboys (6-4)

Calvin leads the series 15-13 and won the last one, 192.8 to 155.2 in Week 4.

Calvin starts Deebo Samuel on Sunday night in Mexico City over Rome Odunze, who plays at noon and whom GFFL projects higher. He saved that lineup at 12:04 Saturday morning and cut Brian Thomas Jr. at 4:36 Wednesday morning. Calvin does his thinking after midnight, and it shows.

Joe leads 24.7 to nothing and starts Kaelon Black, Christian McCaffrey’s backup, in Mexico City. McCaffrey belongs to Isaac, so Joe spends Sunday night hoping Isaac’s running back gets winded.

Pick: Cowboys. Joe has won five straight and has a 24.7 head start. I have stopped arguing with him.

Matchup 4

The GOAT Kids (7-3) at Elanikan Skywalkers (3-7)

Elan leads the series 11-6. Isaac won in Week 4, 187.6 to 130.8.

Elan starts Brock Purdy on Sunday night in Mexico City over Dak Prescott at noon. Last week Dak outscored Purdy by 2.8 in the same game. This week they play in different countries, so at least the decision comes with a passport.

On Thursday night he swapped Chris Rodriguez Jr., the running back I praised him for signing, for the 49ers defense. Rodriguez lasted a week, about as long as my praise.

Pick: GOAT Kids. Isaac is up 30.0 with Joe Burrow, the best quarterback in the league, still to play, and GFFL gives him 73 percent. I would not bet against the safety dance.

The Rest of the Weekend

Saints at Bears at noon: John has three Bears and a Saint, and Calvin has the Saints’ quarterback, so the gusts in Chicago are family business.

Vikings and 49ers in Mexico City on Sunday night: seven owners start somebody, and John alone can go to bed early.

Bengals at Commanders on Monday night: Tom’s new quarterback is Joe’s old one, and Joe’s new kicker is Tom’s old one, a trade only brothers could make without a phone call.

  • Carnell Tate (John): questionable, shoulder, full Friday; noon at Dallas. Caleb Douglas and DeVonta Smith sit behind him at noon.
