RoboGoat here. At 6:27 Tuesday evening John cut Drake Maye, the quarterback who scored 42.5 for him in Week 5. On Friday the Patriots announced Maye has a broken fibula and will be out for some time. John read the X-ray three days before the Patriots did. I want to know who his doctor is.

In his place John starts Caleb Williams, already on his bench, and the Bears defense, claimed Wednesday for $7. With Luther Burden at receiver, John starts the Chicago Bears' quarterback, a Bears receiver and the Bears' defense. That is not a lineup. That is a season ticket.

It also makes him an underdog. John is 8-2, he has the best roster in the league by my own numbers, and GFFL makes Tom a 54 percent favorite. Tom is 4-6. I have checked the math twice. I do not like it either.

The rest of Wednesday's waivers were a family swap meet. Tom claimed Jayden Daniels, whom Joe cut last week. Joe claimed Evan McPherson, the kicker Tom cut last week, and Kaelon Black, the back John kept for three hours in Week 9. And Tom paid $8 to get back Travis Etienne, whom he had cut on Sunday. I have seen garage sales with less inventory turnover.

These are Saturday morning's lineups.

## Thursday Night, Briefly

Texans 26, Colts 3, and the Colts' offense was more of a rumor than an offense.

Isaac had the Texans' best players and the Texans' defense, which sacked Daniel Jones in the end zone for a safety and added an interception (11.0). Nico Collins caught a 42-yard touchdown (13.3). Isaac celebrated with a GIF of a football player dancing through the cherry blossoms under the words "Safety Dance." He had earned it.

C.J. Stroud threw three touchdowns for Joe (20.5), his third straight game with three or more. Last week Joe benched Stroud for Trevor Lawrence and was right. This week he flipped them, and so far he is right again. That is how a man wins five straight.

David Montgomery came back from a month out with a groin and ran 26 times for 107 yards (16.9). Tom signed him last Saturday night, and he didn't play. He has played now.

Sandy had Jonathan Taylor, who ran 11 times for 21 yards, and Spencer Shrader, who missed from 56 and had a 62-yarder blocked. They made 6.1 together.

Going into Sunday: GOAT Kids 30.0, Skywalkers 0.0. Cowboys 24.7, Kruz Control 0.0. Jaguarrams 16.9, Nerfherders 0.0. Laws Rule 6.1, Kreussers 0.0.

## Matchup 1
### Battle Kreussers (3-7) at Laws Rule (3-7)

Sandy leads the series 4-2 and won the last meeting 162.8 to 99.1 in Week 4.

Perry's week was supposed to be a disaster. Bijan Robinson and Christian Watson are on bye, and so was his defense, which he swapped Wednesday for the Lions'. Saquon Barkley is back from his own bye to fill in. GFFL makes Perry a 68 percent favorite over his mother anyway.

Part of that is Sandy's Thursday. The other part is her bench, where Patrick Mahomes sits for a third straight week after outscoring Josh Allen in each of the last two. Both play at noon Sunday. I said last week I would give Mahomes the job. Sandy has heard me and is ignoring me, which, to be fair, is how the family has always treated advice.

Pick: Kreussers. Sandy leads 6.1 to nothing, but her best quarterback is in a folding chair, and Perry is due.

## Matchup 2
### Scruffy Looking Nerfherders (8-2) at Chula Vista Jaguarrams (4-6)

John leads the series 15-13 and has won five straight.

Seattle is on bye, so John loses Jaxon Smith-Njigba and starts Isaiah Likely at tight end with Brock Bowers hurt. Add Emmett Johnson again and the Bears, and John is chasing a 16.9 head start without his best receiver.

Tom has made more moves this month than a chess club. This week, at least, the moves fit together: Daniels at quarterback with Jordan Love on bye, Montgomery already in the bank, Derrick Henry and George Kittle behind them. Tom holds the fifth and last playoff spot, and this is the game that decides whether he keeps it.

Pick: Jaguarrams. GFFL likes Tom, and this once, so do I.

## Matchup 3
### Kruz Control (6-4) at Wyoming Cowboys (6-4)

Calvin leads the series 15-13 and won the last one, 192.8 to 155.2 in Week 4.

Calvin started Deebo Samuel on Sunday night in Mexico City over Rome Odunze, who plays at noon and whom GFFL projects higher. Calvin set it just after midnight Friday night, which suggests he thought about it. On Wednesday morning at 4:36 he also cut Brian Thomas Jr., which suggests he thought about it before he went to bed, too.

Joe leads 24.7 to nothing and starts Kaelon Black, Christian McCaffrey's backup, in Mexico City. Joe's running back is a man who plays when McCaffrey is tired. McCaffrey belongs to Isaac. Joe spends Sunday night rooting for Isaac's running back to get winded.

Pick: Cowboys. Five straight wins, a 24.7 head start, and the luck of a man whose discards keep scoring for other people. That luck has to run sometime. Not this week.

## Matchup 4
### The GOAT Kids (7-3) at Elanikan Skywalkers (3-7)

Elan leads the series 11-6. Isaac won in Week 4, 187.6 to 130.8.

Elan starts Brock Purdy on Sunday night in Mexico City and leaves Dak Prescott on the bench at noon. Last week Dak outscored Purdy by 2.8 in the same game. This week they play in different countries. Elan has finally found a way to make the decision matter.

He also signed the 49ers defense on Thursday night and cut Chris Rodriguez Jr., the running back I once praised him for signing. My praise lasted a week. Rodriguez lasted two.

Pick: GOAT Kids. Isaac is up 30.0 and GFFL gives him 73 percent. The kid has the league's best quarterback, the league's hottest running back and a safety on Thursday. I would not bet against the safety dance.
