Bucky TV setup

Puts the Bucky home screen on the living room TV. About 10 minutes with the TV remote, no computer needed.

Part 1: Install the app

  1. On the TV, open Settings → System → About. Scroll to Android TV OS build and click it 7 times, until it says you are a developer.
  2. Open the Google Play Store on the TV, search for Downloader (by AFTVnews) and install it.
  3. Open Downloader and type this address, including the https:// at the start:
    https://amenfarms.netlify.app/tvapp/bucky-tv.apk
  4. When it finishes downloading, the TV will probably refuse to install it, with a Settings button. Choose Settings, switch Downloader on, press Back, and choose Install.
  5. If Google Play Protect warns that it doesn't know the app, choose Install anyway. It's ours, it just isn't in the Play Store.
  6. Choose Open. The Bucky home screen should appear.
If Downloader says "unknown error": check the address starts with https:// (without it, Downloader is redirected, and some versions give up there), allow Downloader to save files in Settings → Apps → Downloader → Permissions, and delete any older bucky-tv.apk from its Files tab.
No Settings button? The switch is under Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources on most Google TVs, or Settings → Privacy → Security & restrictions on some. It only appears once developer mode is on (step 1), and it is hidden on a kids profile.

Part 2: Make Home open Bucky

Google's home screen stays installed and switched on. Bucky catches the Home button itself, and also steps in when Google's screen appears some other way (turning the TV on, backing out of an app), so Home lands on Bucky from anywhere.

  1. On the Bucky screen, choose the Make Home open Bucky tile. It opens the TV's Accessibility settings. (Or go to Settings → System → Accessibility.)
  2. Choose Bucky TV Home button and switch it on. The TV warns that the app could see the screen; Bucky only watches for Google's home screen and reads nothing on it. Choose OK.
  3. Press Home. Bucky should come up, and the setup tile is gone.
To reach Google's home screen once, hold Home for about a second. To go back to it for good, switch Bucky TV Home button off again in the same place. Bucky stays in the app list.
Don't switch Google's home screen off with a computer (pm disable-user). On this TV, Google's setup screen takes over Home instead and the TV shows a black screen. If that has happened, connect with adb and run adb shell pm enable com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx and adb shell pm enable com.google.android.tungsten.setupwraith.

Using it

  1. The arrow buttons move between tiles, and the center button opens one.
  2. Apps (scroll down) has every other app on the TV, and TV settings at the end.
  3. After 5 minutes without a button press it shows goat photos with the clock. Any button brings the home screen back.
  4. Everything on the screen comes from the website, so changes to Bucky show up on the TV without reinstalling anything. The TV checks for a new version every 10 minutes and switches over the next time the goat photos come on, so it never changes while someone is using it.
