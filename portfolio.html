Scenario model

Projections start from the latest valuation and compound quarterly. Each quarter, in order: growth at the class rate, contributions in, withdrawals out (from cash first), then the management fee on the end-of-quarter balance — the same way the fee is billed in the workbooks. Edit any assumption; everything recomputes and is saved on this device.
